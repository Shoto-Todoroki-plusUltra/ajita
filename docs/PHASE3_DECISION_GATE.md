2. **B1 Ising boundary:** for upper-triangular `E=o+sum Qii xi+sum(i<j)Qij xi xj`, use `xi=(1−Zi)/2`. Verify identity `k=o+sum Qii/2+sum Qij/4`; linear `hi=−Qii/2−sum(j!=i)Q[min(i,j),max(i,j)]/4`; `Jij=Qij/4`. Construct Qiskit `SparsePauliOp`, qubit i mapping to character q−1−i. 3. **Parity gate before QAOA:** original enumerator and CP-SAT verify new fixture references. Across every planned fixture/weight, compare independent original/direct expression, expanded QUBO, classical Ising and SDK basis expectation plus offset. Protocol expects 3,897 basis rows, including N1's classical constant-only case. For SDK-capable tiny cases declare `abs_tol=1e-10`, `rel_tol=1e-12`, reject imaginary residual >1e-10, log worst error/row; integer QUBO/direct expressions require exact equality before SDK floats. A mismatch blocks execution, not a “close enough” result adjusted after inspection.
4. **Bit/layout gate:** one-hot non-palindromic B1 vector `[1,0,0,0]` means B1 display `1000`, SDK display `0001`; it is an invalid original assignment and must remain so. ## Scientific/computational risks that remain open

- **No novelty established:** direct TSP penalty/feasibility studies and school/railway work exist. A configuration difference is not a research gap. This design can support a reproducible replication/negative result only unless later independent research demonstrates more.
- **Tiny and already familiar fixtures:** four deterministic feasible examples, at most nine qubits, with easy exact optima; no claim of out-of-sample or industrial generalization. Accepted examples are not an unseen test set. Paired 20-seed intervals may remain wide; bootstrap is approximate.
- **Scale/optimizer effects:** sufficiency of ground states says nothing about finite-shot shallow QAOA. Hamiltonian normalization fixes one convention, not all optimizer conditioning; results cannot be transferred to other parameter domains, budgets or mixers. Below-bound weights may still be adequate.
- **Accessible space/topology:** B1 is not implemented quantum execution yet. StatevectorSampler lacks mid-circuit measurement support, so Zeno/parity algorithms are not trivial drop-ins. Coefficient-only changes may leave graph/depth identical; no resource-growth premise is assumed.
- **Reliability/missingness:** historical intermittent 20–30-second tiny OR-Tools startup/tail-latency cause remains unresolved. No root-cause fix is claimed. Fresh workers and OS caches complicate “cold/warm” labels. Software deadline/RSS polling can overshoot; abrupt parent/OS termination remains a cleanup limitation. Protocol failures can make confirmatory inference incomplete rather than invite hidden reseeding.
- **Fair baselines/cost:** native OR-Tools/CP-SAT and exact oracles are strong on these tiny instances; a win over random bitstrings is insufficient. CPU simulation is classical work; no inference to quantum energy use, monetary benefit or physical-QPU time. Full setup, compilation, tuning, verification and failure overhead must be retained.
- **Environment uncertainty:** locked historical Mac versions were tested earlier, not rerun now. Current CPU/RAM/free disk/thermal/background load are not newly measured. Multi-platform execution, remote CI and physical quantum noise/hardware remain untested for this design. A source audit does not certify current execution readiness.
- **Deadline/release:** January 2027 application dates are still unspecified; aim for a reviewable smaller result rather than broaden the module. License is unchosen and the project remains unlicensed/unpublished. Cloud, CVRP and public push require separate explicit owner actions.
